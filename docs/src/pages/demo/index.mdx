Lil' Debugger runs on this site. Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd> and hover the cards below. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>L</kbd> to keep it on.

## Nested labels

Hover the button. The panel shows the label of the button, then the label of the card.

<Demo
  html={`<article class="zui-card" data-debug='{"component":"UserCard","userId":42,"plan":"pro"}'>
  <div class="zui-card-body">
    <p>Ada Lovelace</p>
    <button class="zui-button" data-debug="onClick: openProfile(42)">View profile</button>
  </div>
</article>`}
>
  <article class="zui-card" data-debug='{"component":"UserCard","userId":42,"plan":"pro"}'>
    <div class="zui-card-body">
      <p>Ada Lovelace</p>
      <button class="zui-button" data-debug="onClick: openProfile(42)">
        View profile
      </button>
    </div>
  </article>
</Demo>

## Copy a value

Turn the debugger on. Then <kbd>Alt</kbd>+click the badge to copy its value.

  <span class="zui-badge" data-debug="feature-flag: new-checkout=on">
    Beta
  </span>
</Demo>
